# Mobile Stream Hub

Mobile Stream Hub is a **mobile-responsive full-stack web application** developed for **Mobile Phone Sales and Repairs Management**.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔐 Authentication & Security

- Secure login and session management using **JWT with cookies**
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** with authorized routes
- **Password encryption**
- **OTP verification** via email
- Input validation and protected RESTful APIs

---

### 🧑‍💼 Core Management Modules

- Staff Management
- Customer Management
- Category & Item Management
- Cart & Checkout Management
- Order Cancellation Management
- Delivery Management
- Repair Management
- Reorder Handling
- Day-off Scheduling
- Discount & Loyalty Program Management
- Feedback & Rating Management
- Customer Support Management
- Reports Management

#### Integrations

- **Stripe** ([stripe.com](https://stripe.com)) – Online payment processing platform used to securely handle customer payments during checkout.

- **ImageKit** ([imagekit.io](https://imagekit.io)) – Cloud-based image storage and optimization service used to upload, store, optimize, and deliver images efficiently for items.

- **Brevo** ([brevo.com](https://www.brevo.com)) – SMTP-based email service provider used for sending transactional emails such as OTP verification and system notifications.
